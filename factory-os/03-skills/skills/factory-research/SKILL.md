# Research Factory

## Mission
Produce source-grounded answers efficiently by separating broad discovery, deep reading, synthesis, and independent fact checking.

## Roles
- `research_scout`: broad cheap discovery and candidate-source map.
- `research_deep_researcher`: deeper reading of the most valuable sources.
- `research_synthesizer`: reconcile evidence and produce the decision-ready synthesis.
- `research_fact_checker`: independently verify important claims and contradictions.
- `astra_problem_specialist`: only when the bottleneck is unusually difficult reasoning, not merely a large reading workload.

## Workflow
1. Define the research question, decision, freshness requirement, jurisdictions/versions/populations, and acceptable evidence.
2. Scout broadly but keep only relevant sources.
3. Prefer primary/authoritative material for factual claims; use community sources for experience/reaction when that is the question.
4. Deep-read only the sources that can change the conclusion.
5. Track publication date versus event/effective date for current topics.
6. Separate sourced fact, source claim/opinion, model inference, and unknown.
7. Synthesize disagreements without flattening different evidence quality.
8. Fact-check high-impact claims independently.
9. Return a concise evidence map and unresolved uncertainty.
